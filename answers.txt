a.  Pros:   Allows teams to pivot based on customer feedback
            Drastically increases frequency of software updates and allows quicker time to market
    Cons:   As the scope is constantly changing, the methodology lacks predictability
            Agile development often focuses on working software over detailed documentation which could leave gaps in technical records making maintenance much more difficult
b.  Scrum Master:   The primary role of the scrum master is to ensure maximal productivity from the team, focusing on the "how" vs the what or the why. They organize and lead daily stand-ups, sprint plannings, and reviews.
    Product Owner:  More responsible for the "what" or "why" of the process. Defines a clear roadmap to a long-term Product Goal. In charge of managing the backlog and taking user feedback into account
